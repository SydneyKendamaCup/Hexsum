Hexsum Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: Hexsum doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own phone, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

This policy covers the Hexsum app for iPhone and Android, made by Michael Oxley in Sydney, Australia (“we”, “us”).

What Hexsum stores, and where

To remember your progress, Hexsum saves a small amount of game data on your device:

  • which puzzles you’ve solved and the stars you earned
  • moves and hints used on puzzles
  • your daily puzzle streak and streak savers
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and reduce motion

This data stays on your device. It isn’t linked to your name, email or any account, and it’s never uploaded to us. You can erase it at any time in Settings › Reset progress, and it’s deleted when you uninstall the app.

What Hexsum doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No ads.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don’t have any.

Hexsum works fully offline. It uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

App stores

Apple’s App Store and Google Play handle downloads and updates, and may collect information under their own privacy policies, for example crash reports you agree to share with them. We don’t receive personal information from them.

Children

Hexsum doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Hexsum ever adds features that involve data, such as optional ads or purchases, we’ll update this policy before those features are released and change the date at the top.
