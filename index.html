Hexsum Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

In short: We don’t collect your name, email or any account details. Your progress stays on your phone. Hexsum shows optional video ads (you choose to watch one for an extra hint) and offers one optional purchase. Those features use trusted partners, Google AdMob and RevenueCat, who receive limited device and purchase information as described below. If you buy Hexsum Pro, the app shows no ads at all.

This policy covers the Hexsum app for iPhone and Android, made by Michael Oxley in Sydney, Australia (“we”, “us”).

What Hexsum stores on your phone

  • which puzzles you’ve solved and the stars you earned
  • moves and hints used, and how many free hints you’ve used today
  • your daily puzzle streak and streak savers
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and reduce motion

This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to us. You can erase it in Settings › Reset progress, and it’s deleted when you uninstall the app.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra hint after your free hints for the day, or to save a streak. There are no ads between puzzles. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad.

  • On iPhone, Hexsum asks for permission before allowing tracking. If you say no, ads are still shown but aren’t personalised using your activity in other apps.
  • In the UK, EU and some other regions, you’re asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone’s settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Optional purchase (RevenueCat)

Hexsum Pro is a one-off purchase made through Apple or Google, who handle payment. We never see your card details. To confirm and restore your purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random ID created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase history for Hexsum, and basic device and app information. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

App updates (Expo)

Hexsum checks for small content updates, such as new puzzles and fixes, using Expo’s update service. This sends your app version, platform and a random install ID, so the right update is delivered. See Expo’s privacy policy.

What Hexsum doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t receive personal information from these partners beyond aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

App stores

Apple’s App Store and Google Play handle downloads, updates and payments under their own privacy policies.

Children

Hexsum is a general audience game and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Don’t watch optional ads, or buy Hexsum Pro to remove them.
  • Decline tracking on iPhone, or change ad consent where it applies.
  • Ask us any privacy question or request at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page before any change to how Hexsum handles data, and change the date at the top.
